unit weighing matrix

A square $N\times N$ matrix $W$ of complex unimodular elements (including zero) is called a unit weighing matrix if $$WW^{\dagger}=p\mathbb{1}_N$$ where $p$ is its weight [111]. Note that for $p=N$ one gets the well known set of complex Hadamard matrices $\mathcal{H}_N$ of dimension $N$.
