matrix of Butson type

A complex Hadamard matrix of size $N$ is called of Butson type $BH(N, q)$ if all the entries of $H$ are $q^{\rm th}$ roots of unity [51], [52], $$\left(\left[H\right]_{j,k}\right)^q=1.$$

Note that another convention $BH(q, N)$ for Butson matrices is also used in the literature.

Examples:

  • $BH(N, 2)$ all real Hadamard matrices
  • $BH(N, 4)$ contain entries $\pm\{ 1, i\}$ and in the literature are also called complex Hadamard matrices [90]
  • $F_N\in BH(N, N)$ Fourier matrices
  • $F_N\otimes F_N\in BH(N^2, N)$
  • $F_N\otimes F_N\otimes F_N\in BH(N^3, N)$
  • $\quad\vdots$
  • $\bigotimes_{n=1}^kF_N\in BH(N^k, N)$
