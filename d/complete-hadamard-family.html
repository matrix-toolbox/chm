complete Hadamard family

A family of complex Hadamard matrices is called complete if its dimension is equal to the minimal defect over the entire family.

Due to the properties of the defect the complete family cannot be extended.

For example, the family $S_8^{(4)}$ is complete since its defect $$d\left(S_8^{(4)}\right)=4,$$ so it cannot be extended.
