scripts

scripts are available on the GitHub platform https://github.com/matrix-toolbox/

in particular

  • a Matlab script BH.m by S. Budishin including all Butson type matrices
  • a Mathematica script generic4p.nb by F. Szöllősi to generate "random" generic \(G_6^{(4)}\) matrices
  • a \(4\)-dimensional family of complex Hadamard matrices for \(N=6\) can be generated numerically with the Mathematica 6.0 code F6Foray.nb designed by A. Skinner et al.
  • a Matlab/Octave and Mathematica scripts to calculate defect of unitary matrix
