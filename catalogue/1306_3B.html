Extension of $M_{13C}$ to a $2$-parametric (maximal) affine family:

$$R_{M_{13C}^{(2)}}(b, c)=\left[\begin{array}{rrrrrrrrrrrrr} \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet\\ \bullet & \bullet & c & b & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & c & \bullet & \bullet & b & \bullet & \bullet\\ \bullet & \bullet & c & b & b & b & \bullet & c & b & b & b & b & b\\ \bullet & \bullet & c & b & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & c & \bullet & \bullet & b & \bullet & \bullet\\ \bullet & \bullet & c & b & c & c & \bullet & c & c & c & b & c & c\\ \bullet & \bullet & c & b & b & b & \bullet & c & b & b & b & b & b\\ \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet\\ \bullet & \bullet & c & b & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & c & \bullet & \bullet & b & \bullet & \bullet\\ \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet\\ \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet\\ \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet\\ \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet\\ \bullet & \bullet & c & b & c & c & \bullet & c & c & c & b & c & c\\ \end{array}\right]$$
